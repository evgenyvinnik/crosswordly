Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The Crosswordly iOS app does not collect any data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and your progress never leaves your device.

The website at crosswordly.ca uses Google Analytics to count visits and see how levels are played.

Crosswordly is a word puzzle game made by Evgeny Vinnik, an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Crosswordly app for iPhone and iPad or play at crosswordly.ca.

The iOS app

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information or usage data. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • Progress stays on your device. Completed levels, your language and your settings are saved only in the app's storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Works offline. Every level, word list and font is built into the app, so it does not connect to the internet while you play.
  • Sharing is up to you. When you tap Share or Download, the iOS share sheet opens and you choose where the link or image goes. We never receive what you share. A shared link contains only the puzzle and its solution, nothing about you.
  • Links to other sites. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn or crosswordly.ca open in your browser, where those sites' own privacy policies apply.

The website (crosswordly.ca)

  • Progress stays in your browser. Your progress and settings are saved in your browser's local storage. Clearing your site data removes them.
  • Google Analytics. The website uses Google Analytics to understand how many people play and which levels they finish. It records page views and game events such as starting a level, completing a level, and sharing or opening a shared puzzle. Google also collects standard information like your approximate location, device and browser type, and sets cookies with a random identifier. This information is not used to identify you or for advertising. You can read how Google uses this data in Google's partner sites policy and block it with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or your browser's tracking protection.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to keep its service secure, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Crosswordly is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no information, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 or anyone else.

Your choices

There is no account and no stored data about you to access or delete. To reset your progress, delete the app or clear crosswordly.ca's site data in your browser.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game are welcome as an issue on GitHub.
